## English

- Fixed an issue where minimizing the game during High & Low could leave only the title bar visible. High & Low now pauses capture while the game is minimized and resumes when the game window is restored to a usable size.

## 繁體中文

- 修正比大小執行期間，遊戲最小化後可能只剩標題列的問題。遊戲最小化時會暫停擷取，等遊戲視窗還原到可用大小後再繼續。
